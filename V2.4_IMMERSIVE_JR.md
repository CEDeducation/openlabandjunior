# OpenLab v2.4 — Immersive Jr. Science Studio

## What is truly implemented

New full-width Jr. OpenLab UI, not a separate app. The OpenLab Research workspaces and Supabase configuration remain unchanged.

### Ten interactive prototype labs
1. **Atomic Explorer** — 118 clickable elements, neutral and charged shell models for atomic numbers 1–20, live electronic charge changes.
2. **Ionic Bonding** — drag/tap a valence electron from Na to Cl, Na⁺/Cl⁻ charge and shell feedback, reset.
3. **Molecule Composer** — click/tap/drag shared electron pairs to build H₂, O₂, N₂, H₂O and CO₂ with independent bonds and checking.
4. **Reaction Engine** — coefficient controls, element-wise live conservation for four balanced-reaction exercises.
5. **Cell Microscope** — clickable schematic organelles in plant/animal cells, inspect descriptions.
6. **DNA Sequence Lab** — editable strand, draggable/tappable A-T/C-G complements, check and reveal.
7. **Food Chains & Pyramids** — assemble chains for grassland, pond or ocean with tap/drag, check against expected sequence, display illustrative trophic energy pyramid.
8. **Human Digestion** — clickable organs and staged food journey, correct distinctions between alimentary canal and accessory organs.
9. **Electric Circuit** — voltage/resistance sliders, series resistor toggle, open/closed switch, live I=V/R and power readout.
10. **Ray Optics** — incidence/reflection controls, Snell-law refraction, total internal reflection when applicable.

### Other UX
- Original visual design with dark simulation canvas, bright reading inspector, searchable science library, sidebar, and learning journal.
- PC and tablet oriented, mobile layout fallback.
- Notes and completed experiment flags are stored per profile in the same browser only (not Supabase/cloud sync).
- Existing English/Traditional Chinese selection remains, but translation coverage of the newly built labs is **not complete**.
- Current NCERT Class 9–12 data is a **topic roadmap**, not verified full syllabus content.
- This is a prototype with a limited set of scientifically motivated interactive teaching models, **not** full molecular dynamics or detailed 3D anatomy.

## Deploy the update to existing v2.3

Replace/add only these files, preserving paths:

- `app/junior/page.tsx` (replace)
- `components/junior/JuniorStudio.tsx` (new)
- `components/junior/junior-labs.tsx` (new)
- `components/junior/studio.css` (new)

You do NOT need to replace `components/junior/school-data.ts`; however, your existing file **must contain the v2.3 TypeScript fix**: `pairs: number[][]` rather than `pairs: [number,number][]` in `positions()`.

If you cannot update only these files, the full ZIP is provided as an alternate.
